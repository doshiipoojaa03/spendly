Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect any information beyond what is needed to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to let you sign in, record and review your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only through your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may delete your expenses or your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may make requests to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch with the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
